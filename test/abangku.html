A3 — STEP 1 is the whole experiment

This is a lens-closing control, not a likely finding. Step 1 opens a popup whose navigation hangs forever and then does nothing at all — no paint, no write, no stop(). Whatever the pill shows over that blank popup tells you, in one tap, whether Arc ever sources the omnibox from a pending popup URL. If it does not, the entire popup/pending class is closed and you can stop.
